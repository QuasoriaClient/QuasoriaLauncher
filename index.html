How it works

  1. 01

    Pick a version

    Pick any version, from Indev and Alpha up to 26.3. Your last pick is saved in this browser.

  2. 02

    Choose a runtime

    For 1.8.8 and 1.12.2, choose WebAssembly for better frame rates or JavaScript for older devices.

  3. 03

    Launch

    The game opens on eaglercraft.com in a new tab. Your worlds are stored in that browser.
